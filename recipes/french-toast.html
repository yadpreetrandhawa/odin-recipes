French Toast

Description

French toast is made by soaking slices of bread in a mixture of beaten eggs, milk, and warm spices like cinnamon or vanilla, then cooking them in a buttered pan until the edges turn golden and crisp. The inside stays soft and custardy, creating a rich contrast with the lightly caramelized exterior. Served warm, often topped with syrup, powdered sugar, or fresh fruit, French toast delivers a cozy, sweet breakfast experience that feels both simple and indulgent.

Ingredients

  • 3 Eggs
  • 2/3 cup milk
  • 2 slices bread
  • Butter
  • Maple Syrup

Optional

  • Blueberries
  • Banana

Directions

  1. Make the egg whisk
    • Whisk eggs, milk and cinnamon
  2. Soak the bread in egg mixture
  3. Pan fry the french toast
  4. Plate with berries and banana slices
